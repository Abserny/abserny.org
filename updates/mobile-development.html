Core Architecture Complete

All Systems Integrated

All major systems of the Abserny mobile app have been implemented and integrated into a single working application ahead of the v1.0 release.

What was completed

FSM supervisor

The deterministic finite state machine governing BOOT, READY, SCANNING, SPEAKING, and ERROR states is fully implemented. Race conditions between gesture events and ongoing scans are structurally prevented.

Bilingual Gemini prompts

Separate Arabic and English prompt templates for each of the four detection modes are finalized. Prompting in the target language produces significantly more natural spatial phrasing than translation.

Offline fallback

Silent automatic failover to ML Kit image labeling and OCR when offline or when the Gemini API is unavailable. Tested at 380 ms median latency with 0% failure rate across 50 trials.

Interactive onboarding

The voice-first onboarding sequence — spoken language picker followed by interactive gesture tutorial — is complete. Every step can be completed without looking at the screen.

Spoken Settings menu

Triple tap opens a fully spoken settings overlay, navigated by swipe, confirmed by double tap. Language change and tutorial replay are both accessible here.
