Abserny Announced

Graduation Project Kickoff

Abserny (أبصرني, "See Me") is officially announced as a graduation project at the Faculty of Specific Education, Computer Science Department, Academic Year 2025–2026.

Project vision

The goal is a mobile application that lets visually impaired users understand their surroundings through gesture control and spoken AI descriptions — no screen interaction required at any point, including during setup.

Design principles established

  • Accessibility first. Every decision evaluated from the perspective of a user who cannot see the screen.
  • Voice before visual. Audio output is the primary channel. Gestures are the only input method.
  • Reliable by design. A deterministic FSM governs all interaction. Offline detection ensures the app always works.
  • Bilingual. Full Arabic and English support at every layer from day one.

Technology decisions

React Native with Expo SDK 54 selected for cross-platform reach. Gemini 2.0 Flash Lite selected as the AI backend for its latency, accuracy, and free-tier availability. ML Kit selected as the offline fallback for its reliability and zero-configuration on-device operation.
